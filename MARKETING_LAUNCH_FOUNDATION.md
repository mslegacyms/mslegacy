# MS Legacy — Marketing Launch Foundation

This document outlines the brand launch copy templates, positioning guidelines, and initial marketing calendar for introducing MS Legacy.

---

## 1. Brand Launch Positioning
MS Legacy is positioned as a **premium, metrics-driven property wealth advisory and real estate stewardship platform**. 
We focus on:
*   *Verification-First Sourcing*: Traceable parent deeds, infrastructure analytics, and RERA compliance checking.
*   *Turnkey Construction Responsibility*: Engineering management, budget transparency, and structural clear-offs.
*   *NRI Portfolio Custody*: Hands-off local caretaker management.
