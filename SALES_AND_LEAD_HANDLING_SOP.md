# MS Legacy — Sales & Lead Handling SOP

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the operational SLA targets, calling scripts, and CRM logs rules for advisors.

---

## 1. Live Operational Rules (SLAs)
Advisors must adhere strictly to these live response guidelines:
*   **15-Minute Response**: Every new web lead must be contacted within **15 minutes** during working hours (9:00 AM - 6:00 PM IST).
*   **Max 24h in New Stage**: No lead should remain in the "New" column on the Kanban board for more than **24 hours**.
*   **Logging Obligation**: Every client communication (call, WhatsApp, email) must be logged instantly using the Activity Composer in the CRM dossier.
*   **Mandatory Next Tasks**: Every qualified lead must have at least one active follow-up task scheduled with a due date.
*   **Escalation Threshold**: Founder escalation is required immediately for all HNI, NRI, co-development, and high-value land parcel opportunities.

---

## 2. Lead Handling by Segment

### A. NRI / International Leads
*   *Action*: Issue a structured advisory email response within 2 hours, attaching the corporate credentials brief.
*   *Verification*: Set priority to **High** in the lead record.

### B. Legal Review Leads
*   *Action*: Tag the lead for "Documentation Review" in the status selector.
*   *Verification*: Schedule a deed verification task assigned to our legal desk representative.

### C. Channel Partner Leads
*   *Action*: Verify their RERA license ID on the Karnataka RERA register page before approving their partner account status.
