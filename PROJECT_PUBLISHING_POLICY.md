# MS Legacy — Marketplace Project Publishing Policy

This policy document governs when, how, and under what conditions developer projects and promotional content are published on the public discovery marketplace.

---

## 1. Visibility States Controls

*   **Draft**: Default state for all imported, scraped, or manually typed layouts. Hidden from the public website.
*   **Published**: Visible in the marketplace list view. Can only be set after Step 6 (RERA Check) and Step 7 (Permission Checks) of the SOP are passed.

---

## 2. Core Restrictions Policy

1.  **Zero-Logo Policy without Permission**: Do not serve developer logos unless written email consent is logged in the permission tracker. Use the text badge replacement instead.
2.  **No Copyrighted Floor Plans**: Do not upload brochure images or floor plan blueprints unless supplied directly by developer sales managers under authorized marketing kits.
3.  **Strict RERA Veracity**: Do not publish RERA certification statuses as approved unless the registration ID matches active entries in the Karnataka RERA registry.

---

## 3. Review Cadence
All published project entries must undergo a **bi-weekly compliance check** to verify:
*   Availability updates.
*   Possession timeline updates.
*   Takedown requests from promoters (must be resolved within 24 business hours).
