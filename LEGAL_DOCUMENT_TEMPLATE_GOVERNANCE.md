# Legal Document Template Governance & Policy

## Purpose
This document outlines the governance and compliance protocols for creating, editing, and publishing property-related document templates within the MS Legacy Legal Drafting Engine.

## Legal Safety Policy

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All agreement templates generated by the drafting engine are **draft layouts for review purposes only**. They must never be represented to clients, sales agents, or partners as final execution-ready or binding legal contracts without verified counsel.

1. **Safety Watermark**:
   All documents that have not attained the status of `"approved_for_print"` must display a clear visual watermark:
   `DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW`
2. **Mandatory Footer Warning**:
   All printouts and PDF files must contain the following disclaimer:
   *“This template is generated for documentation preparation only. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Verify title, authority approvals, stamp duty, registration requirements, RERA applicability, tax implications, and local laws before execution.”*
3. **Specific Category Warnings**:
   - **Sale Deed**: Must mention that the format must be finalized by an advocate and executed/registered as per local stamp duty and registration rules.
   - **GPA / SPA**: Must remind users of notarization and local registration mandates depending on powers granted.
   - **Rental / Lease**: Must indicate that tenancy rules, stamp duty, and police verification vary by city and duration.

## Versioning & Review Cycle
All templates must follow a strict semantic versioning structure:
- **Major Version (X.0)**: Substantial structural changes (e.g. adding structural indemnity clauses). Requires signoff by two Advocate officers.
- **Minor Version (0.X)**: Minor text updates, typos, or local city adjustments. Requires signoff by one Admin or Advocate officer.
