# MS Legacy — Compliance Copy Audit Report

This report summarizes the marketing copy audit completed to eliminate regulatory risks, ensuring compliance with RERA and SEBI advertising regulations in India.

---

## 1. Audited Pages
All public-facing routes have been reviewed:
*   Homepage (`/`)
*   About Us (`/about`)
*   Services portal (`/services`) and sub-divisions.
*   Opportunities listings (`/opportunities`)
*   Project Participation page (`/project-participation`)
*   Property Wealth Score details (`/property-wealth-score`)

---

## 2. Risk Copy Cleanups & Replacements
We completely removed legally sensitive investment terminology:

| Legally Sensitive Phrase (Risk) | Clean Replacement (Compliant) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Guaranteed ROI / Assured Profit | Structured Project Participation |
| Fixed Returns / Assured Returns | Risk-Reviewed Asset Opportunities |
| Buy Shares / Invest Now | Sourcing Audits / Request Review |
| Risk-Free / Double Money | Independent Legal Due Diligence Required |
| Public Investment Scheme | Private Placement / Invitation-Only Review |

---

## 3. Safe Project Participation Terminology
Public portals now frame co-development participation using RERA-safe phrasing:
*   Opportunities are labeled as **Private Placement / Structured Participation**.
*   All offerings are framed as **private opportunities subject to eligibility reviews**.
*   Includes clear warnings: *MS Legacy does not guarantee returns on capital, specific exit timelines, or property appreciation.*

---

## 4. Public Disclaimer Locations
*   **Site Footer**: Active across all routes, containing the general corporate disclaimer stating that listings represent demonstration entries and that buyers must seek independent legal counsel.
*   **Project Participation Portal (`/project-participation`)**: Contains the explicit private placement notice and warnings regarding municipal layout dependencies.
*   **Property Wealth Score Portal (`/property-wealth-score`)**: Contains the notice that scores represent decision-support tools and do not constitute financial guarantees.

---

## 5. Pre-Launch Recommendation
*   **Final Legal Review**: Before launching the production server, have a local real estate attorney review all terms of service and agreement templates.
*   **Listing Verifications**: Verify that all future property listings have active Karnataka RERA numbers before switching them to public visibility.
