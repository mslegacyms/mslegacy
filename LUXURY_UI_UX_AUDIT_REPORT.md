# MS Legacy — Luxury UI/UX Design & Architecture Audit

This audit evaluates the visual aesthetics, styling architecture, grid consistency, trust elements, and command center ergonomics before initiating the premium visual upgrades.

---

## 1. Audit by Dimension

### A. Color & Brand Systems (Generic vs Luxury)
*   *Current State*: Mapped color variables for deep navy (`#0B132B`) and gold (`#C5A880`). Warm ivory backgrounds and thin borders are defined.
*   *Luxury Gap*: Spacing scale needs to be adjusted. Background sections require subtle linear gradients (such as architectural blueprints grids or land contours) to present a premium feel.

### B. Typography & Scale
*   *Current State*: Configured Outfit (Sans-Serif) for body copy and Cormorant Garamond (Serif) for headers.
*   *Luxury Gap*: Headline line-heights are slightly crowded. Increase line-height on `.font-serif` headings and add tracking adjustments (`tracking-wide`, `tracking-wider`) on uppercase category badges.

### C. Homepage Impact (Emaar/DAMAC Benchmark)
*   *Current State*: Standard grid section with service directories and Wealth Score lists.
*   *Luxury Gap*: Needs a high-impact cinematic dark hero section, trust numbers strip, and structured advisory process steps to feel like a flagship wealth desk.

### D. Service Pages & Sourcing Showroom
*   *Current State*: Standard landing descriptions.
*   *Luxury Gap*: Needs a structured service promise, process timelines, and targeted NRI/HNI trust points.

### E. CRM Command Center
*   *Current State*: Clean tabular layouts with sidebar menus.
*   *Luxury Gap*: Needs spacing adjustments on tables, custom gold outline badges, and clearer headers.

---

## 2. Priority Upgrades Queue
1.  **Refine CSS Tokens**: Introduce soft gradients, blueprint borders, and card elevations.
2.  **Cinematic Home Hero**: Implement full-screen dark editorial banners.
3.  **Opportunities Showroom**: Create gold contour gradient placeholders.
4.  **Forms polish**: Style advisory intake inputs with focus accents.
