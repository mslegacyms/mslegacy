## 2. Project Comparison Matrix (`/project-compare`)
*   Supports up to 3 projects side-by-side.
*   Compares location corridors, price brackets, RERA approvals, unit types, and lists custom risk check pointers and advisory advice.

---

## 3. Sourcing Calculators Center (`/calculators`)
Integrated 8 reusable planning calculators:
1.  **EMI Calculator**: Estimates monthly loan installments.
2.  **Affordability Calculator**: Maps monthly income to safe acquisition budgets.
3.  **Stamp Duty & Registration Calculator**: Computes estimated transaction duties.
4.  **Rental Yield Calculator**: Computes gross and net yield rates.
5.  **Plot Cost Calculator**: Projects plot acquisition totals.
6.  **Construction Cost Calculator**: Projects civil construction rates and interiors costs.
7.  **NRI Ownership Planner**: Projects foreign currency outflow targets.
8.  **Shortlist Scorecard**: Grades assets based on legal title clarity and RERA registrations.

*Disclaimers*: A standard planning notice is embedded under every calculator card.
