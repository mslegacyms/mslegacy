# Project Data Collection Form Fields

Use these fields to configure internal data entry forms, Google Forms, or Typeforms.

---

## 1. Form Sections

### Section 1: Developer Information
1.  **Developer/Promoter Name** [Text, Required]
2.  **Authorized POC Name** [Text, Required]
3.  **POC Contact Email** [Email, Required]
4.  **POC Contact Phone** [Phone, Required]

### Section 2: Project Parameters
5.  **Project Name** [Text, Required]
6.  **Project Type** [Select: Apartment | Plotted | Villa | Farmland | Township]
7.  **Construction Status** [Select: Ongoing | Completed | Upcoming | Pre-launch Watchlist]
8.  **RERA Number** [Text]
9.  **RERA Certificate Upload** [File upload, PDF only]
10. **Locality / Region** [Text, Required]
11. **Google Maps Link** [URL, Required]
12. **Project Acreage** [Text, E.g., 25 Acres]
13. **Total Units** [Number]

### Section 3: Configurations & Media Clearances
14. **Configuration Summary** [Text, E.g., 2, 3 BHK Apartments]
15. **Starting Price (INR)** [Number]
16. **Logo Permission Confirmation** [Checkbox, Required]
    *   *Label*: "I authorize MS Legacy to display our corporate logo for promoter identification."
17. **Brochure Permission Confirmation** [Checkbox, Required]
    *   *Label*: "I authorize MS Legacy to host our marketing brochures for verified customer downloads."
18. **Floor Plan Permission Confirmation** [Checkbox, Required]
    *   *Label*: "I authorize MS Legacy to display standard unit layouts."
19. **Accuracy Declaration** [Checkbox, Required]
    *   *Label*: "I declare that all RERA registration statuses and approvals listed are accurate."
