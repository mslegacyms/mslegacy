# MS Legacy — Founder Launch Review Checklist

This checklist is designed for the Founder / Executive committee to execute a final quality check before launching the website.

---

## 1. Brand Alignment
- `[ ]` **Typography**: Google Fonts Outfit & Cormorant Garamond load cleanly without browser default fallbacks.
- `[ ]` **Color Aesthetics**: Navy and Gold color schemes present a McKinsey/Deloitte-level advisory feel.
- `[ ]` **Image assets**: Visual templates are placed securely; no default wireframe templates remain on public view.

---

## 2. Regulatory & Legal Review
- `[ ]` **Safe Vocabulary**: Check that public routes have zero instances of "Guaranteed Returns", "Fixed ROI", "Double your money", or "Assured Profit".
- `[ ]` **Disclaimer Visibilities**: General disclaimers are present on `/project-participation`, `/property-wealth-score`, and footer bands.
- `[ ]` **Eligibility Frames**: Co-development projects are framed as invitation-only co-development reviews.

---

## 3. CRM & Portals Check
- `[ ]` **Lead Capture**: Forms capture city, country, preferred budget, and advisor interests correctly.
- `[ ]` **Advisor Dossiers**: Advisor logs notes on timelines and reviews checklist tasks.
- `[ ]` **Client Vault**: Clients can review consultation updates and checklists safely without cross-data exposure.
- `[ ]` **Broker Approvals**: Broker applications require RERA license identifiers.

---

## 4. Final Verification
- `[ ]` **Mobile responsive checks**: Checked layouts on mobile viewports.
- `[ ]` **SEO parameters**: Page metadata description parameters are active.
- `[ ]` **Go / No-Go Decision**: Committee signs off on DNS release.
