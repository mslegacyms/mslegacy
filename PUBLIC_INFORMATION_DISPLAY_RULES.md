# Public Information Display Rules

This document governs the rules for rendering unverified public-information project records if previewed in our system.

---

## 1. Core Display Policies

*   **Disclaimer Requirement**: Any public-information project must clearly render the disclaimer banner linking to `/project-disclaimer`.
*   **Verification Status**: The RERA badge must display `Verification Pending` or `Public Information` unless manually confirmed against the Karnataka RERA registry.
*   **No Unapproved Logos**: Do not display developer logos unless written release agreements are logged in the CRM database.
*   **No Unapproved Media**: Do not host brochures, floor plans, or site photos unless written media releases are logged.
*   **Price Indicators**: All pricing brackets must be marked as *"Editable Planning Assumptions"* or *"Pricing Estimates"* and are not final.
*   **Wording Restrictions**: Strictly prohibit guaranteed ROI, appreciation promises, or risk-free marketing vocabulary.
*   **Actionable CTAs**: All buttons must lead to *"Request MS Legacy Advisory Review"* or *"Request Due Diligence Support"*. Gating calls to action like "Book Now" or "Buy Unit" is strictly prohibited.
