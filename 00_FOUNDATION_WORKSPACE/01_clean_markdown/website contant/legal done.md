RERA Information
Commitment to Regulatory Compliance
MS LEGACY supports transparency, accountability, and consumer protection within the real estate sector.
We encourage all property buyers and investors to verify the registration status of applicable projects under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act (RERA).
What Is RERA?
The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act was introduced to improve transparency, accountability, and efficiency within the real estate industry.
The Act aims to protect buyers and promote responsible development practices.
Why RERA Matters
RERA helps ensure:
Project Transparency
Developers must disclose project information and updates.
Buyer Protection
Improved safeguards for homebuyers and investors.
Regulatory Accountability
Developers are required to comply with applicable regulations.
Improved Information Access
Greater visibility into project approvals and registration details.
Our Commitment
Where applicable, MS LEGACY encourages verification of:
Project registration status
Developer compliance
Regulatory approvals
Documentation requirements
through the relevant state RERA authority before making investment decisions.

Independent Verification
Buyers and investors should independently verify project registration details through the applicable state RERA website and regulatory authority.
MS LEGACY can assist clients in conducting RERA verification as part of our Legal Services Division.
