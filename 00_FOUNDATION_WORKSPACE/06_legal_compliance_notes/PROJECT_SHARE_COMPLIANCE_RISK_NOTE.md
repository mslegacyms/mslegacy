# Project Share — Compliance & Legal Risk Note

**Prepared for**: MS Legacy Executive Committee  
**Subject**: Regulatory Alignment for the "Project Share" & "Fractional Ownership" Models  
**Jurisdiction**: India (SEBI, Companies Act, RERA)

---

## 1. Context & Regulatory Risks of "Shares" Language

The term **"Project Share"** is highly effective from a marketing standpoint, but it carries significant regulatory risks under Indian corporate and securities laws if not carefully positioned.

### A. SEBI Collective Investment Scheme (CIS) Risks
Under Section 11AA of the SEBI Act, 1992, any scheme or arrangement where capital pooled from investors is managed on their behalf to generate returns can be classified as a **Collective Investment Scheme (CIS)**. 
*   *The Risk*: Operating an unregistered CIS is a severe offense in India. If the public website promotes pooling investor funds to purchase land and develop layouts, SEBI can issue regulatory actions, freeze assets, and halt operations.
*   *Threshold*: Any pooling arrangement involving 100 or more investors or a fund size of ₹100 Crores or more is automatically audited as a potential CIS, even if structured via private agreements.

### B. Companies Act, 2013: Public vs. Private Placements
If MS Legacy issues actual shares (equity or preference shares) in a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) to developers and investors:
*   Any offering made to more than 200 persons in a financial year is legally classified as a **Public Offer**.
*   Public offers require filing a detailed prospectus with the Registrar of Companies (ROC), which is complex and expensive.
*   Advertisements on a public website offering "shares in layout projects" can be interpreted as an illegal public solicitation for a private placement.

### C. RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) Constraints
Under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, advertising a project layout or soliciting bookings before registering the layout with RERA is strictly prohibited. Offering pre-launch investment opportunities on the public website before securing RERA registration can result in severe financial penalties from Karnataka RERA.

## 2. Recommended Public Copy Transitions

To mitigate these compliance risks, the public website must use terminology that aligns with private partnership and asset co-ownership structures.

| Do Not Use (Risk Areas) | Safe Alternatives (Compliance-Aligned) | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Invest in Project Shares" | "Structured Project Participation" | Avoids implying corporate share issues. |
| "Guaranteed ROI / Fixed Returns" | "Target appreciation horizons / Project-specific exit timelines" | Eliminates the promise of guaranteed financial returns. |
| "Crowdfunding / Pool Capital" | "Private co-development opportunities / Joint-venture models" | Avoids SEBI CIS classification. |
| "Fund managers / Real Estate Fund" | "Development partners / Turnkey executors" | Avoids AIF (Alternative Investment Fund) registration requirements. |
| "Buy Land Shares" | "Asset co-ownership / Fractional land banking participation" | Frames the transaction around property co-ownership rather than financial securities. |
| "Public Investment Pool" | "Eligibility-reviewed project participation" | Limits solicitation to private, qualified participants. |

## 3. Items Requiring Formal Legal Review
Before the development phase, the company's legal counsel must review the following structures:
1.  **Entity Structuring**: Define whether project participations will be structured as Private Limited SPVs, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), or Registered Co-Ownership deeds (Deeds of Undivided Share).
2.  **Private Placement Limits**: Establish systems to ensure that project participations do not exceed 200 members per LLP/SPV in a financial year to comply with the Companies Act.
3.  **Client Onboarding (KYC)**: Implement KYC checks to verify identity, PAN cards, and banking channels to ensure compliance with the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA).
4.  **RERA Alignment**: Ensure that no specific layout is advertised or offered for participation until the developer has secured its RERA registration number.
