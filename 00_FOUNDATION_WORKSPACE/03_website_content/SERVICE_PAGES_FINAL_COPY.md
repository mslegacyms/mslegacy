## 4. Legal Due Diligence Services

*   **Header Title**: 
    > "Institutional-Grade Real Estate Legal Services"
*   **Core Value Statement**: 
    > "Securing your property transactions with title investigations, municipal compliance checks, and clear legal documentation."
*   **The Problem**: 
    > The largest risk in real estate is incomplete due diligence. Unregistered parent deeds, outstanding bank loans, property tax defaults, or missing municipal permissions can lead to costly legal disputes.
*   **The MS Legacy Solution**: 
    > Our Legal Services division conducts detailed pre-purchase investigations. We compile clear title reports, trace ownership histories for up to 30 years, verify khata documents, and draft secure agreements to protect your investments.
*   **Due Diligence Packages**:
    *   **Comprehensive Title Search**: "Review of the 30-year ownership history, mother deeds, and registration records."
    *   **Khata & Revenue Verification**: "Auditing property khata certificate validity (A-Khata/B-Khata) and mutation records."
    *   **Encumbrance Certificate (EC) Auditing**: "Verifying that the asset has no outstanding mortgages, liens, or court disputes."
    *   **Agreement Drafting & Registration**: "Drafting Sale Deeds, Sale Agreements, and Power of Attorney documents, with complete registration support."
*   **Call-to-Action**: `Order a Legal Title Verification Report ➔`
