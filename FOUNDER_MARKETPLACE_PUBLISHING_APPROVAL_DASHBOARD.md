## 2. Decision Workflow Rules
1.  **Audits Trail**: Every status toggle requires writing audit notes detailing the RERA registry lookup date and next actions check parameters.
2.  **Approve Public Information Preview**: Triggers public render of the layout card with text-only placeholders and verification disclaimers active.
3.  **Approve RERA Record Matched Preview**: Updates badge status to `RERA Record Matched` and unlocks standard verification check fields.
4.  **Send to Legal Review**: Automatically flags the record to block public render and alert the legal panel.
5.  **Keep Internal / Draft**: Ensures the project remains restricted to `/crm/projects` views only.
