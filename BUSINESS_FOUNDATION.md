## 3. Target Customer Segments
MS Legacy targets high-value real estate buyers and investors:

1.  **Residential Home Buyers**: Families, individuals, and first-time buyers looking for legally verified plots, luxury villas, apartments, or custom-built homes in emerging growth corridors.
2.  **HNI & Wealth Investors**: High-Net-Worth Individuals looking for capital appreciation through strategic land banking, rental assets, or passive project-share investments.
3.  **Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)**: Overseas professionals seeking hassle-free real estate acquisitions, virtual site inspections, secure legal representation, and property management/rent-collection services.
4.  **Land Owners & JVs**: Owners of agricultural or commercial land seeking developmental expertise and funding partners for joint layout development.
5.  **Channel Partners (Brokers)**: External agents seeking reliable inventory, professional sales backing, and transparent commission disbursements.

## 4. Service and Product Portfolio

### A. Property Sales
*   **Residential Plots & BDA Sites**: Premium, government-approved sites with high appreciation potential.
*   **Luxury Villas & Townships**: Independent houses and gated communities.
*   **Commercial Plots & Office Spaces**: Grade-A commercial assets, retail shops, and mixed-use spaces.
*   **Agricultural & Farm Lands**: Managed farm communities and agricultural land banking.

### B. Turnkey Construction Services
*   Complete architectural planning, structural engineering, government approval coordination, interior design, construction management, and final handover.
*   **Asset types built**: Luxury villas, farmhouses, independent duplex homes, warehouses, commercial retail centers, and industrial facilities.

### C. Property Management
*   Complete end-to-end landlord services: tenant sourcing, strict background checks, leasing documentation, rent collection, routine maintenance, utility payments, and virtual monitoring for overseas owners.

### D. Legal & Documentation Services
*   Title verification (ownership history, parent deeds, title search reports).
*   Khata verification (A-Khata, B-Khata, mutation records).
*   Encumbrance certificate (EC) auditing (checking liens, mortgages, or court disputes).
*   Registration support, Sale Deed drafting, and Power of Attorney (POA) execution.

### E. Project Share Investments (Wealth Platform)
*   **Equity Participation**: Investors acquire shares in layout developments, land banking, villa construction, or warehouse setups.
*   **Structured Models**: Revenue sharing, fixed annual returns, or JV participation with clear exit timelines (e.g., 1 to 5 years).

## 5. Revenue Model
MS Legacy generates revenue from multiple streams across the property lifecycle:

*   **Transaction Commissions**: Percentage of sales value on residential, commercial, and land deals.
*   **Development & Construction Margins**: Turnkey build contracts and PMC (Project Management Consultancy) fees.
*   **Asset Management Fees**: Recurring property management fees (typically 8-10% of monthly rental income or flat annual caretaking fees).
*   **Legal Service Fees**: Fixed-rate packages for title verifications, documentation drafting, and registration handling.
*   **Project Development Profits**: Equity returns and developer margins from layouts and construction joint ventures funded via Project Share Investors.

## 6. Value Proposition & Competitive Differentiation
*   **The Problem**: The Indian real estate market is highly fragmented. Buyers must deal with unverified land agents, unreliable builders, slow legal advisors, and independent property managers, resulting in massive risk, legal disputes, and financial loss.
*   **The MS Legacy Solution**: An all-in-one platform providing **Zero-Risk Real Estate**. Every property is audited using MS Legacy's proprietary **PROPERTY WEALTH SCORE™** framework before recommendation.
*   **Data-Driven Approach**: Shifting the purchase decision from emotional speculation to logical analytics based on:
    *   *Location Score* (Growth corridors, infrastructure, upcoming Metro).
    *   *Legal Score* (Title clarity, RERA status, litigation tracking).
    *   *Infrastructure & Connectivity Score* (Access, roads, economic hubs).

## 7. Legal & Compliance Requirements
Because MS Legacy operates in the highly regulated Indian real estate market, it must adhere to strict guidelines:
*   **RERA Compliance**: All project layouts, apartments, and villa developments advertised must list active RERA registration numbers. Channel partners must also be RERA registered.
*   **Stamp Duty & Kaveri Online Integration**: All property transactions must comply with Karnataka stamps and registration laws, requiring secure links to Kaveri, Bhoomi (RTC/Pahani check), and e-Swathu (rural property khata) portals.
*   **KYC / AML Laws**: Under Indian financial laws, client onboarding for transactions and project shares requires full KYC verification (Aadhaar, PAN, and passport/OIN for NRIs).

1.  **Investment Tiers & Regulatory Structuring**: The Project Share model (fractional ownership/syndication) needs clear legal framing under Indian SEBI guidelines or company registration parameters to ensure compliance.

3.  **Property Wealth Score Weighting**: The mathematical algorithm defining the weightage of each scoring category (e.g., Location 30%, Legal 35%, Infrastructure 20%, Connectivity 15%) needs a clear template.
4.  **Corporate Office & Registry**: Registered address, incorporation documents, and GST numbers must be provided for the corporate branding elements.
