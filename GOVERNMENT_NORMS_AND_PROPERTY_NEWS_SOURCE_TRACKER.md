## 2. Verification Protocol for Content Managers

1.  **Direct Citations**: Never copy full third-party articles. Write original summaries based on official circulars.
2.  **Verification Tags**: Always set appropriate tags:
    *   `official_source`: Direct circular or gazette notification linked.
    *   `verified_summary`: Summarized from official authority updates.
    *   `advisory_note`: General risk warning checklist compiled by MS Legacy.
3.  **Disclaimer Mandate**: All posts must display the regulatory disclaimer warning that norms are subject to change.
